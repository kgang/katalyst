**Summary**

**Representation.** A causal Bayesian network over time-ordered, resolvable binary propositions is the right core: it factorises, so local edits are cheap. Scenario/event trees are exponential and unfactored — use them only as a derived "top-k paths" view. Argument maps belong as evidence annotations, never as probability carriers. Add one decision + utility node at the very end (a minimal influence diagram) to produce the tradeable thesis. Pearl's ladder must be visible in the UI: `observe` (conditioning, propagates upstream and explains away), `do` (edge-cut, no upstream revision), and structural edit are three different numbers. True rung-3 counterfactuals about resolved history need a latent-threshold parameterisation, and should be labelled "model-dependent". With LLM-elicited structure you can honestly claim rungs 1–2 *relative to the asserted model* only; both the optimistic (Kıcıman) and pessimistic (Zečević, "causal parrots") literature agree the model recalls causal claims rather than discovering them.

**Parameterisation.** Signed log-odds links as default, not noisy-OR — noisy-OR cannot express inhibition ("already priced in", "OPEC offsets"), which is ubiquitous in finance. Keep noisy-AND for conjunctive chains and noisy-MAX for ordered quantity nodes.

**Elicitation.** Base rate before narrative, conditionals never joints, k≥5 samples aggregated by geometric mean of odds, no extremizing (chain errors compound), sample dispersion retained as free parameter uncertainty. ForecastBench still shows superforecasters at Brier 0.068 vs 0.119 for the best LLM.

**Cheap high-value rigor.** Parameter-level Monte Carlo band, one-way tornado, per-edge "load-bearing" ablation badges, and VOI-ranked tripwires. Skip imprecise probability entirely.

**Inference.** Variable elimination for numbers, forward sampling for the multiverse narrative, plus a test asserting they agree.

The report ends with a 10-item minimal core, 8 rigor traps each with a concrete reproducible test (conditioning-as-intervention, path multiplication, zero leak, LLM-stated joints, base-rate swamping, among others), and an explicit do-not-build list.</result>
