## Q2 — Why "This happened" puts nothing on the change list (`diff.md` open question 7)

**In one sentence:** when you say an event actually happened and the map moves, the tool currently says nothing moved — is that because we ran too few worlds, or because we are counting something wrong?

**It is a counting mistake, and I measured it.** Observing that Brent settled below \$68 on the Hormuz map, at the shipped 8 worlds per version:

- **209 of the 2,000 versions have no surviving world at all.** Those versions report a likelihood of `0.0` (`backend/src/katalyst/domain/propagation.py:1327-1330`) and count for exactly nothing in the number and the band, because their weight is zero (`propagation.py:1177-1178`).
- **Then they are counted as votes against the direction.** `_agreement_on` takes a plain unweighted mean of the signs (`backend/src/katalyst/domain/diff.py:598-599`) and never looks at those weights.
- 209 ÷ 2,000 = **10.45%**. The measured shortfall below the 90% bar is **10.95%** (M1 and M2 both read 0.8905). **Among the versions that actually survived, agreement is 99.44%.**

So the whole of open question 7 is dead versions voting. The reported *move* is survival-weighted; the reported *direction* is not. Two populations, one comparison.
