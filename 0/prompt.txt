# Part C — tensions nobody has named

## C1. The tile is 272 pixels tall in code and "at most 216" in the chapter

`tiles-ports-wires.md` proposes a clamp of 96–216 px, and `INV-workbench.1`'s test asserts exactly that. `/Users/kentgang/git3/katalyst-wt/feat-03b-canvas/frontend/src/graph/geometry.ts` ships `TILE_HEIGHT = 272` — *fixed*, not clamped, with a comment arguing the opposite case ("a map of boxes that are all the same height reads as a layout"). One of them is wrong and a written test fails either way. **Take the code's position** — uniform height is the better argument and it is what the screenshots show — and change the chapter to "fixed at 272, a multiple of 8" plus checklist line 8. Cheap now, a test failure later.

## C2. A supposed claim still gets a `Belief` of `1.0` that nothing is allowed to print

While a supposition holds, `World.beliefs[H]` must carry *something*, and the only legal something is `p = lo = hi = 1.0`. `Belief` permits it, `belief_out_of_range` does not catch it, and INV-7 is satisfied. Every surface is told to read `states` first and print the word — but the outline view, the delta rail and the summary sentence are three more readers, and one of them will print `1.0` eventually. **Say it once in `propagation.md`:** a claim whose state is `supposed` carries `1.0` so the path product has a factor to multiply, and no surface but the path product may read it. One sentence, and `beliefChip.test.tsx` already has the matching test.

## C3. "Agreement" means two different things, and one of them is about to be a column heading

FR-8 uses *agreement* for run-to-run disagreement across generations (stack 04). Record 0014 and `diff.md` use it for the share of the 2 000 versions that moved the same way. `STATUS.md` flagged this on 17 September and `diff.md` B3 flags it again — neither resolved it, and 03b is about to paint the word on screen as a rail column. Renaming a heading after stack 04 ships is worse than renaming it now. **Head the column "same direction"** — plain words, says exactly what it is — and leave *agreement* free for FR-8. The field name can stay `agreement`; field names are not on screen.

## C4. `Diff.summary` has no sentence for a diff in which nothing shifted

`summary` is a required `str` filled from a template whose first slot is "the top row of the rail". INV-multiverse.27 quietly dodges the empty case by quantifying only over pairs "with at least one row". A branch that moves no terminal — perfectly reachable with a `believe`, or a `retune` below the 0.005 floor — leaves the template unfillable. **Add the second template sentence:** *"&lt;edit&gt; moves no ending and leaves &lt;n&gt; claims untouched."* It is also the most honest answer that branch can get, and it needs no new field.

## C5. A claim's resolve-by day may not survive the 180-point series cap

A tile's headline is read on the claim's own resolve-by day; the series is sampled down to 180 evenly spaced points when the window is longer. Those two rules do not coordinate, so on a long map a tile's headline number would be a point that does not appear anywhere on that tile's own sparkline. `INV-multiverse.24` already solved the analogous problem for the delta row (`at_day` is always a day the series carries) — the tile needs the same guarantee. **Sample down, but always keep every claim's resolve-by day among the points kept.** One clause in `propagation.md` B1. Hormuz's window is sixty days so it never bites today; a generated map in stack 04 will.</result>
