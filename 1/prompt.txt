## What I chose, and why

**One wrapping line.** The far-away belief line stopped being a three-column grid and became a flex line that wraps: `market ▬ .074` on the first line, `.029–.081` under it — and **only when it will not fit**. The wrapped range is indented to line up with the number, not the owner (a hanging indent: `padding-left: calc(var(--far-owner) + var(--space-1))` with the owner pulled back out by the same distance), so it reads as the rest of that belief rather than a belief of its own. Nothing counts beliefs, nothing measures anything — the browser wraps when the words run out of room. That is the "one rule".

It has to wrap: `market` (78.4 px) + gap + bar + `.074` + gap + `.029–.081` = **281 px** against **256** (244 on `hypothesis`/`not_tradeable`). At 22 px nothing closes a 37-pixel gap — I checked dropping letter-spacing (5 px), halving the gaps (8 px) and dropping the bar (16 px): 252 px, still over on a cut tile, and all three are losses.

**Where the second line's room came from** (the box is reserved from the full form and may not grow):

| Reclaimed | px | Why it was free |
|---|---|---|
| The bar stopped defining the line's baseline | 5.3 / line | The bar carries no text, so the browser used its bottom edge as the whole reading's baseline; a 4 px bar centred in a 22 px line sits ~5 px above where the words sit, pushing every belief line down for nothing. `.belief-chip__figure { align-self: baseline }` — the bar does not move. |
| Belief line-height 1.25 → 1.15 | 2.5 / line | Far-form only. |
| Claim↔beliefs gap `--space-2` → `--space-1` | 8 once | The near form already uses one step. |

**What I rejected:** the claim giving up its second line when there are two beliefs (`:has()`). It buys only a hypothetical case, by 0.1 px — no margin at all — and costs half the claim on the map's most important tile on *every* two-belief map. Sizing the owner's column to its own word was also rejected: the worst case *is* `market`, so it saves nothing there, and it would knock two beliefs on one tile out of alignment.

**One thing the first fix got wrong that I found:** the owner's column was pinned at `3.5em` = 77 px, and `market` is 78.4 px. In a grid the word simply overflowed its track (part of why the line ran over); the moment I made it a flex item it printed **`mark…`**.
